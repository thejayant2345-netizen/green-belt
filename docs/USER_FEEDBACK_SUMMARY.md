## 💡 Key Insights & Feature Requests Collected

1. **Auto-Goal Locks**: 4 users requested a time-locked savings option (e.g. lock savings until school tuition due dates in September).
2. **Multi-Asset Support**: Pilot senders asked for direct support for EUR and AED stablecoin pairs alongside USDC.
3. **SMS Alerts for Receivers**: Feature request to send WhatsApp/SMS notifications to non-smartphone family members when funds arrive.
